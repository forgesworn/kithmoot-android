# Android notifications, 16 September 2026

## Messages as they are sent, 1 October 2026

Message notifications now work like a phone's own messages:

- **On by default, previews included.** Message notifications, previews and "Receive messages when KithMoot is closed" all default to on. Settings already saved on a device keep their saved value, so a phone where previews were left off still needs that one switch turned on. The lock screen still sees only "New message" unless Android is set to show private content.
- **From the background service.** A saved room watched while KithMoot is closed now shows each new message as it arrives. The service already decrypted and verified the message to count it, so a notification is now posted from that same message. Only messages the inbox counts (someone else's, new, not an edit, reaction, retraction or invitation) notify. The text goes into Android's notification when previews are on, is otherwise held in the service's memory, and is never written to storage: `BackgroundInbox` still stores none.
- **One notification per room**, in Android's conversation style: the latest six messages with their senders. A two-person conversation is titled by its sender, any other room by its name. Each room's notification is tagged with its id and its tap intent carries the id in its data, so rooms neither replace each other nor share a tap target. Opening a room clears its notification.
- **Heads-up.** Messages use a new channel, `chat_messages_v2`, at high importance, because a channel's importance cannot be raised after creation. The old `chat_zen_v1` channel is deleted, with any sound or importance a person had set on it.
- **Asked for once.** On Android 13 and later the notification permission is asked for the first time a room opens, with an explanation first, then the battery-optimisation exemption. Without the exemption Android suspends the background connection soon after the screen goes off.
- **Tapping goes to the conversation.** It opens the room on its Chat tab at the latest message. If a different room is open it is left first. If that room is on a call, the notified room opens beside the call instead.

- **Reply from the notification.** A room's notification has a Reply action, as Signal's does: type, send, and KithMoot never opens. It is Android's inline reply (`RemoteInput`, marked as a reply action, no screen of its own) and needs the phone unlocked, because a locked phone shows only "New message" and must not be able to speak in a room. Its PendingIntent is mutable, as inline reply requires, names `NoticeReplyReceiver` explicitly (not exported), and carries the room in its data, so rooms never share one.
  - **Offered only where a reply can be sent with nobody to ask** (`canReplyFromNotice` in `notifications/NoticeReply.kt`). A reply is the same chat event an open room sends: signed by this device's own key under a device credential that already exists, sealed to the room's current epoch, with no change to the protocol. A room whose key is held here mints that credential itself; a paired device uses its pairing's; a room joined as an account uses the credential kept from its last opening (`SavedRoom.headlessSigning`), so the signer app is never asked. No Reply, and tapping opens the chat as before, for anonymous rooms (Tor only), quiet rooms (they send on their own schedule through their box), ended rooms, rooms this device has been removed from, rooms that need a Kindred proof, rooms joined as a bunker account, rooms joined as an account that is no longer the one signed in, and any room whose credential has less than five minutes left (an account room's kept credential lasts a day from when it was minted, or seven days for a Ring me room, which is renewed once half of that is gone; an opening reuses it while twelve hours remain, so Reply lasts at least twelve hours after the room was last open, and for a Ring me room whose signer answered recently it lasts days; or a lapsing pairing). The bunker exclusion follows the background service's; a kept credential would in fact sign without the bunker.
  - **Sent through the room if it is open.** A room open in the app registers its send path (`OpenRoomReplies`, beside `ActiveRoomRegistry`), and a reply to it goes through that session, exactly as the composer sends. Otherwise it goes out over the background service's own connection for that room, or, when the service is not watching it, over a short-lived connection to the room's own relays and no others, closed straight after.
  - **Never lost.** As in the room, the signed message is kept, encrypted, in the room's pending-message slot before it is first published, and cleared only when a relay confirms it. A receiver has ten seconds, so a reply waits eight for a relay. Unconfirmed, it stays kept and goes when the room next opens or the background service next reconnects, and the notification says "Not sent yet. Kept on this phone: open KithMoot to retry." A reply that could not even be kept (another message is already waiting, the room ended meanwhile) shows its whole text on the notification with "Not sent. Open KithMoot to retry.", so it can be copied.
  - **Afterwards.** The notification is posted again, silently, with the reply at the end as a message from "You" (with previews off, "Reply sent" rather than the text), which also stops Android's spinner. Replying marks the room read in the background inbox, so the next message notifies on its own; in the open room the notification shows the reply until something new arrives or the room is read. The text is never logged; the log names the room by a digest and the outcome only.

Not checked on a phone: the permission and battery prompts, heads-up display, how quickly a message arrives with the screen off, and the tap from a cold start. For Reply: the action appearing and the inline field opening, the unlock prompt from the lock screen, the spinner stopping, the reply showing as "You" in both a room and a two-person conversation, delivery with KithMoot closed and the background service off (the short-lived connection), a Link relay that asks for authentication (the short-lived connection has no signer for it, so only the service's own connection can confirm there), and a slow network leaving the reply kept and then sent when the room opens.

## Staying reachable

A Ring me room joined as an account needs a device credential, signed by the account's signer, for the phone to answer a call. `service/CredentialRenewal.kt` renews it quietly, and when it cannot and a credential is within two hours of lapsing it says so in two places (ringing itself needs no credential; answering does): a notification ("You can't answer KithMoot calls yet", channel `reachability_v1`, "Answering and ringing calls") and a banner, on the rooms list and in the room, which stays until the condition clears and has one button, "Confirm with My Signet" (or "your signer" when the signer's name is not known). The banner does not depend on the notification, which is easy to dismiss. Both use the same condition (`roomsAtRisk`). A Ring me room's credential lasts seven days and is renewed below three and a half; a room credential has no maximum lifetime on any verifier (only the person form is capped, at thirty days).

The notice is not the only route, and it is not the only thing that can go quiet. The banner follows the saved rooms and their credentials, never whether a quiet signer session could be opened; the notification is posted again whenever quiet renewal ends without fixing it (nobody signed in aside), including for a bunker, an account that cannot be read and a signer that has to show a screen. The foreground screen refreshes the banner without re-posting a notification the person has just dismissed. Account menu, Notifications & sound, carries the same button as a second permanent route.

### Ringing switched off

The "Listening for calls" notification's action was "Turn off": one silent tap that switched off both ringing and background message delivery, with the only way back buried in Settings. It is now **Stop ringing**, it is shown only while ringing is on, it stops ringing and leaves message delivery as the person had it (the service keeps running for messages, or stops if there is nothing else to do), and it posts a follow-up, "Calls won't ring while KithMoot is closed", with a **Turn back on** action that stays until it is used or swiped away. The old action string is kept so a notification already in the tray from an older build still works.

While ringing is off and any saved room is set to Ring me (`ringingSwitchedOff`), a banner on the rooms list and in a Ring me room says "Calls won't ring while KithMoot is closed." with one button, **Turn on**, which switches ringing on and starts the listener. The same row is at the top of Notifications & sound. The banner shows however ringing was switched off, including deliberately in Settings; set the rooms to Notify quietly or Nothing, or switch the room's ringing off, to clear it. The listener cancels the follow-up whenever it finds ringing on, so every way of turning it on clears it.
