# Signet contacts and private conversations

Existing Signet/NIP-46 and Android signer sign-in remains the account entry
point. From the account menu, **Contacts** links a separate Signet v2 grant
for names, public keys and blocks. Sign-in does not itself grant directory
access. The person approves in Signet, enters the code KithMoot displays,
and confirms the pairing in KithMoot. No new identity or room is created by
linking the directory.

Search names or npubs locally, then press **Message**. No introduction room
or shared roster is required. A persistent room admits exactly the two account
keys and retains the existing chat, calls and collaboration behaviour. Repeated
Message taps reuse the earliest known conversation for the pair, including
authenticated invitations received on the account inbox. Concurrent first
messages on different devices can still create two rooms; choosing the earliest
known room converges future taps, without deleting either history.

## Invitation transport

This matches the browser's `src/contact-invitation.ts`. A NIP-17 kind-14
unsigned rumor contains the existing persistent invitation URL, with exactly
`["p", recipient]` and `["kithmoot-invitation", "1", roomId]`. The account
signs a kind-13 NIP-44 seal. A fresh ephemeral key wraps that seal in kind 1059,
whose only tag is its recipient. The authenticated sender, rumor hash and
exact two-member admission policy must agree before an invitation is offered.
The room derived on admission must also match the advertised room ID.

Both the recipient and sender receive a copy. Kind 10050 selects inbox
relays, with NIP-65 receiving relays and configured relays as fallbacks for
outgoing invitations. This application fallback is not a complete general
NIP-17 messenger implementation. Ordinary NIP-17 chat from other messengers
is ignored; conversation messages continue through the KithMoot room protocol.

The contact list and invitation journal use independent account-scoped
Android Keystore/AtomicFile vaults. Exact signed envelopes are saved before
delivery. Each relay acknowledgement clears only its corresponding outbox
entry. Failed delivery remains visible with **Retry invitation delivery**;
restarting does not silently discard it or create another room. Account
replacement, revoked grants, current blocks and removed invitations are
checked again at delayed relay dispatch. Removing an account room also
withdraws its retained invitations. Dismissal is durable, and never joins,
starts media or runs collaboration actions.

The initial inbox reads at most 256 wraps from 32 days, with a 64-envelope
receive buffer, 256 retained invitations, 128 pending deliveries, 2,048 seen
IDs/dismissals and a 2 MiB plaintext journal. These bounds deliberately do not
promise a complete historical account archive. Initial invitations are
discovered while the account is open in the app. Existing saved-room Android
background call/message listeners continue to handle joined conversations;
this change does not add a new background invitation notification service.

## Contacts authority and SDK

The unmodified shared Kotlin contacts core is pinned in
`sdk-signet-contacts/vendor/source.json`, with an archive SHA-256 checked
before compilation and its MIT licence included in the jar. No sibling checkout
or unpublished Maven package is required. The native adapter supplies the
existing signer, verified relay transport and exclusive encrypted storage.

Directory scopes and grant binding are enforced independently of sign-in.
Known blocks survive expiry/revocation. Expired directory names may remain
usable for 30 days, matching the browser's names-only policy; they carry no
tier or verification authority. Malformed/unreadable block caches fail closed.
Self contacts are hidden and conflicting names do not decorate a key.

## Verification

`ContactInvitationsTest` opens committed browser-generated envelopes and
exports native envelopes to `app/build/interop/contact-invitations-android.json`.
The browser's `scripts/contact-invitation-interop.mjs verify <file>` opens
both native envelopes. `app/src/test/resources/contact-invitations-web.json`
is generated by that browser script and copied verbatim; its repeated-byte
keys are public test identities only.

`ContactInboxTest` covers restart/retry with exact envelopes, independent
acknowledgements, duplicate delivery, durable dismissal, grant withdrawal and
room removal during delayed dispatch, storage refusal and account mismatch.
`SignetContactDirectoryTest` covers stale/expired names, sticky blocks,
revocation, grant/scopes/future-time refusal, self/ambiguous names and cache
corruption. `SignetContactsUiTest` runs the installed Compose journey through
real sockets, Keystore, private-room admission and chat: contact-first creation,
offline sender, recipient reply, same-room reuse and Activity restart. It uses
synthetic already-confirmed grants, and is included in the ordinary recovery
emulator matrix.

Real Signet approval with an owner's directory and a physical external signer
remain manual acceptance gates. Unit/emulator results do not establish those
gates, store publication or deployment.
